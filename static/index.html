What will be cropped
Axial CT and contours are cropped along the head–foot axis around structures named PTV* or CTV*. Subtraction helpers such as “Bowel - PTV” are excluded.
The default margin is 15 cm above and below. Pixel values are unchanged. New matching Study, Frame, Series and image UIDs are created by default.
Download the cropped CT, RTSTRUCT and crop report from Jobs. Review the retained image range and contours.
